Why GameManor?

YOUR COLLECTION. YOUR RULES.

GameManor Launcher helps you manage your PC game collection in one organized desktop interface, without bloatware, advertisements, or storefront lock-in.

Centralized Personal Library

Organize games across Steam, GOG, Epic, itch.io, emulators, and standalone directories under one unified, responsive launcher.

Local SQLite Storage

All your records, play sessions, and settings reside in a local SQLite database on your machine. Your library data remains 100% private.

Accurate Session Tracking

Process monitoring tracks when your game starts and stops, calculating playtime down to the second without background telemetry.

Direct Executable Launching

Configure working directories, launch parameters, and custom binaries. Games start with direct OS execution rather than vendor wrappers.

Flexible Customization

Switch between gaming dark mode and crisp light mode. Manage system tray minimization, Windows startup, and customizable view filters.

Safe Data Management

Integrated backup creation, integrity verification, and restore tools make safeguarding your gaming library effortless.

Simple Setup

HOW IT WORKS

Get your desktop game vault organized in three straightforward steps.

01

Add Your Games

Use Smart Game Import to scan your installation folders automatically, or add titles manually with custom artwork and paths.

02

Organize Your Collection

Group titles into PC Games, Emulators, or VR. Tag favorites, mark planned downloads, and verify drive connection status.

03

Launch & Track Playtime

Launch games with one click. GameManor monitors the active process, records your session duration, and updates your playtime statistics.
